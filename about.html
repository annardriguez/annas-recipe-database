About the kitchen

Not another sad recipe folder.

I built Anna's Kitchen Index because my recipes were living everywhere: screenshots, saved posts, notes, random links, and the occasional memory I definitely could not be trusted to keep.

I wanted one friendly place where I could find meals fast, compare the useful stuff, save favourites, and plan the week without making food feel like admin.

So that is what this is: a warm, practical little kitchen index for hungry days, busy days, and the days where picking dinner is somehow the hardest task on earth.
