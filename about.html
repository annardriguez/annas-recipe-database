About the kitchen

A recipe drawer for real-life appetite.

Anna's Kitchen Index is for the recipes that survive actual life: screenshots, saved posts, notes app chaos, grocery-store panic, and the occasional meal you swear you will remember and absolutely will not.

It is built to help you decide faster, save the good stuff, and plan just enough without turning dinner into a productivity seminar.

The rule

Food should feel possible.

This is not a perfect wellness museum. It is a practical, pretty place for meals with protein, comfort, shortcuts, and enough structure to make future-you slightly less annoyed.
